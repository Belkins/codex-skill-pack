# Visual Direction Rubric

Choose one coherent direction and execute it precisely.

## Must Decide

- Theme: light, dark, editorial, product-led, immersive, playful, enterprise, or category-specific.
- Typography: display and body hierarchy, avoiding generic sameness when the brand permits.
- Asset role: product screenshot, live demo, image-led narrative, outcome artifact, or user context.
- Motion: none, subtle, or product-state-driven.
- Density: sparse, balanced, or information-rich.

## Fail Conditions

- Looks like generic dark AI SaaS by default.
- Uses decorative blobs/orbs as the main visual idea.
- Relies on card stacks without a page narrative.
- Hides the actual product until far below the fold.
- Makes mobile users scroll past brand, promise, CTA, or proof before understanding the offer.
