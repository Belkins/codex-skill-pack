# Objection Patterns

Cover the objections that match the offer:

- Time: how fast can the user try or see value?
- Price: what is free, paid, refundable, cancellable, or included?
- Trust: why believe the product works?
- Privacy: what happens to sensitive data/audio?
- Setup: what is required before trying?
- Fit: who is this not for?
- Quality: what makes the output reliable?
- Alternative: why not use the incumbent or a human service?

Write answers as page copy, not internal notes.
