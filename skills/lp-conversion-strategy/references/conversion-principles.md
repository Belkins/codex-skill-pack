# Conversion Principles

## Strategy Gate

Before copy or design, the page needs one clear answer for each question:

- Who is the page for?
- What urgent moment brought them here?
- What result do they want?
- Why is this product the credible path?
- What proof can they inspect before committing?
- What is the next action?

If any answer is broad, the page should become a segment page or the strategy should pick a sharper segment.

## First Viewport

The first viewport must carry five signals:

1. Category or offer.
2. Concrete user or use moment.
3. Outcome or relief.
4. Proof or inspectable product evidence.
5. One primary action.

## Proof Ladder

Prefer proof in this order:

1. Actual product demo or interactive state.
2. Measured product metric from the user's system.
3. Screenshot of the result/outcome.
4. Customer quote with real attribution.
5. Comparison table with sourced claims.
6. Founder/company claim clearly marked as a claim.

Do not invent metrics or testimonials. Mark missing proof as needed.
